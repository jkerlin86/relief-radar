# Narrative-selection audit

## Scope

Complaints received 2015-01-01 to 2026-08-14 — the window in which a published narrative was possible. Outside it, 'no narrative' is structural, not a consumer choice, and including those complaints would manufacture a selection effect.

## How big is the narrative layer?

3,836,347 of 16,782,339 primary-population complaints (22.86%) carry a narrative. The share is not stable over time: it is 32.39% in 2015 and 22.42% in 2025 (the archive thins sharply as publication ends in 2026; see figures/narrative_share_by_year.png and reports/parts/selection_by_year.csv). Narrative rates also differ by product, channel, and company size — full tables in reports/parts/selection_by_*.csv.

## Do narrative-bearing complaints end differently?

Raw relief rates: 2.58% with a narrative vs 0.73% without. That raw gap mixes composition (which products and eras produce narratives) with any within-cell difference.

Inside 132 year x product cells, the volume-weighted mean relief-rate difference is 0.22% and the median cell difference is 0.34%; narrative complaints have the higher rate in 76% of cells.

## Conclusion

The narrative layer can speak for the complaints that carry narratives — a self-selected, opt-in minority whose mix of products, eras, and channels differs measurably from the full stream. It cannot speak for all complaints: any statistic computed on narratives alone inherits that selection, and the layer closes entirely after August 2026, so narrative-based monitoring has no future data. Findings from the narrative arm of this project are reported as findings about narrative-bearing complaints, full stop.
